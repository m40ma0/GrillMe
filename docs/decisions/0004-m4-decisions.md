Date: 2026-10-29. No stack changes. New dependencies (worker): `vega` 6.4.0, `vega-lite` 6.4.3, `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, `pdf-lib` 1.17.1, `@aws-sdk/s3-request-presigner`; `@stage-ready/db` now depends on `@stage-ready/ai` (the seed scores fixture takes with the same code as the worker). ## Evidence Q&A Kit (F4)

1. **Question bank.** The `questions` job gathers criteria, the claim ledger of the last three sessions (with evidence), repo findings, personas, the latest Coach Note's open and new items, and measured step latency, and asks Muse Wonder (`questionBank.v1`) for 20 to 30 questions, each with likelihood, damage, source refs and an answer card. The schema enforces at least 20 questions and 55 words per script (`ANSWER_MAX_WORDS`); a failure retries once with the validation error. The mocked adapter builds the bank deterministically (`heuristicQuestionBank`): contradicted claims first, unverified numbers, unshown features, repo TODOs and hard-coded responses, measured steps, criteria by weight, persona questions (only when the persona's style is itself a question), and a fixed set of hard generic questions.
2. **Evidence refs are checked, not trusted.** A card may only cite refs that exist in the data (`latency:<step>`, `criterion:<name>`, `repo:<file:line>`, `claim:<text>`); anything else is dropped. Rebuilding keeps drill history (`askedCount`, `lastScore`) for questions with the same text.
3. **Gap to task.** A question whose card has no evidence and a suggested artefact becomes a Fix Task (`origin: question`), once per question.
4. **Slides.** Numbers come only from a `dataSnapshot` built from stored data (`buildSlideSnapshot`). Charts are Vega-Lite compiled to SVG (`vega` with the `none` renderer), nested inline in a 1920×1080 SVG template and rasterised with resvg. (A chart embedded as an `<image>` lost its text: resvg loads fonts only for the main document.)
5. **Muse Image composition is deferred** (first in the spec's cut order after the synthesis features, "keep deterministic slides"). Its endpoint and request shape are not confirmed in the Meta docs, so every slide uses the deterministic template. `MODEL_IMAGE` stays reserved.
6. **Verification.** Muse Spark reads the rendered PNG (`slideVerify.v1`) and lists every number; each number in the snapshot must appear. A mismatch, or a failed verification call, renders the deterministic fallback template (numbers printed as large figures, no chart) and marks the slide `fallback`. The mocked adapter stands in for a perfect reader of the text the template drew, so in Demo Mode verification passes unless the template drops a number.
7. **PDF appendix** is rebuilt after each slide run (one 16:9 page per slide, ranked by likelihood × damage) and stored at `projects/<id>/qa-kit/appendix.pdf`.
8. **Auto-refresh.** `session-review` queues `questions` when it finishes, so new rehearsal data rebuilds the bank and its slides.
9. **Drill.** Questions are drawn with `pickDrillQuestion` (weight grows with likelihood × damage, weakness and never-asked status). Answers are recorded in the browser and transcribed by the vision service (`POST /transcribe/upload`, faster-whisper); Demo Mode plays a strong or weak fixture answer instead. `scoreDrillAnswer` scores length (≤ 55 words, ≤ 20 s), directness (no hedge opening, answer-first overlap with the card) and evidence (a number or the card's evidence).
10. **Cmd+K finder.** The palette lists every rendered slide by question and preloads the PNGs when the app shell mounts, so a slide is on screen well under 2 s (the e2e test asserts it).

## Best-Take Director (F5)

11. **Take score** is `scoreTake` with weights from `TAKE_SCORE_WEIGHTS` (default 0.30/0.30/0.20/0.20, −0.15 per Contradicted claim, floored at 0). Contradicted claims in a take come from the deterministic auditor rules against measured latency, the repo and the written docs, and are listed in the score breakdown. Demo success is 1 until DemoGuard incidents exist (M5).
12. **Takes** are recorded in the browser (screen, optional camera picture-in-picture composited on a canvas, mic), uploaded straight to storage with a presigned PUT, then the `take-process` job transcribes them (vision `POST /transcribe`, word timestamps) and scores them. Recording takes requires the project's cloud upload switch: the server needs the media.
13. **Section plan edits keep takes.** Saving the plan used to delete and re-insert `section_plans`, which cascaded to takes; both writers now upsert by `(project, key)`.
14. **Auto-EDL and length fitter.** `buildAutoEdl` takes the best valid take per section, then swaps in the tighter take that loses the least score per second saved until the cut fits the limit. Output length counts the 2 s intro and outro cards and the overlap at each join.
15. **Transitions.** Each clip has a transition in: a 200 ms crossfade, or a "cut", which is a 40 ms dissolve so audio never clicks. The outro always crossfades. Captions, chapters and the expected duration use the same overlaps as the renderer.
16. **Title cards** (intro, outro and chapter lower-thirds) are drawn deterministically with Pillow, not Muse Image, for the same reason as slides. They are the only generated frames; every clip is the user's own take.
17. **Render** runs on the vision service (`POST /render`, polled at `GET /render/{id}`): per-clip trim and normalise to 1080p30 and 48 kHz stereo (ultrafast near-lossless intermediates), an `xfade`/`acrossfade` chain, a loudness measurement pass on the joined audio, then one final encode with burned ASS captions, two-pass `loudnorm` to −14 LUFS (linear), libx264 `FFMPEG_PRESET` (default veryfast) and AAC. The worker uploads SRT and chapters itself and gives the service presigned PUT URLs for the MP4 and thumbnail. Pytest asserts a valid MP4 within 100 ms of the expected duration at −14 ± 1 LUFS.
18. **Demo Mode fixture takes** have no recording: the renderer draws labelled colour bars ("Demo Mode fixture take") with a tone, at 540p, scaled up in normalisation. The 12 fixture takes are seeded with transcripts and scores, and an auto-EDL that fits 180 s (171.1 s).
19. **Render time.** On this 4-core dev container the full 171 s Demo Mode cut renders in 198 s (measured end to end through the worker), inside the 4-minute budget; most of it is the final x264 encode. Real takes skip the fixture synthesis. Lower `FFMPEG_PRESET` if a slower host misses the budget.
20. **Render jobs live in one service instance's memory.** A poll that lands on another Modal container returns 404 and the render fails with a clear message; scale the render function to one container per job, or move job state to Redis, before scaling out.
21. **Thumbnails per take** are not generated yet (the take grid shows a placeholder with the duration); the rendered video's thumbnail is exported.
22. **J / K** move between takes of the active section; the take breakdown shows on hover or keyboard focus.

## Not verified in this environment

Real faster-whisper transcription (model download blocked here), the Meta API for Wonder and Spark, and MinIO itself (a moto S3 emulator stood in locally; CI runs MinIO). FFmpeg here is 6.1; the spec names 7, and nothing used needs 7.
